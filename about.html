About AI QUIZ BATTLE

AI QUIZ BATTLE is a free quiz game where you play against NEXbrain, a friendly AI character. It is made to help students and families learn General Knowledge, Science, Maths and English while having fun. The game works in Hindi, English and Nepali and can be played by kids, teenagers and adults.

▶ Play now

How to play

  1. Choose who is playing: Kids (6–12), Teens (13–17) or Adults (18+). Kids get easier questions and extra protection.
  2. Pick a mode: Teacher (learn with explanations), Battle (race against NEXbrain) or Smart AI (NEXbrain switches between teaching and competing).
  3. Answer each question within 15 seconds. After every answer you see the correct option, a simple explanation and an interesting fact.
  4. Earn coins for correct answers, finish levels 1 to 10 and collect stars and achievements.

What you can learn

GK – countries, capitals, mountains, rivers and world facts, with many questions about Nepal and South Asia.
Science – the human body, space, chemistry basics and physics ideas.
Maths – quick calculation, percentages, LCM/HCF, geometry and number patterns.
English – vocabulary, grammar, spelling, idioms and opposites.
Photo Quiz – flags, animals and places shown as pictures.

Why explanations matter

A wrong answer is a chance to learn. The game explains why the correct answer is right, gives a memory trick when possible and adds one new fact, so players remember more than just the answer.

Coins, levels and rewards

Coins are only for fun inside the game. They can be used for power-ups such as 50-50 and extra time, and for avatar skins in the shop. Coins have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.

Safe for families

Your game progress and your profile photo stay on your own device. For Kids mode, sign-in, the online leaderboard, invites and coin bets are switched off. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
