Frequently Asked Questions

Is AI QUIZ BATTLE free?
Yes. It is free to play in your browser. The site may show ads to help cover costs.
Which languages are supported?
Hindi, English and Nepali. You can switch language in Settings.
Is it safe for children?
Yes. In Kids mode (ages 6–12) sign-in, online leaderboard, invites, friend challenges and coin bets are switched off, and Analytics is not loaded. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
Do coins have real money value?
No. Coins are only for fun inside the game. They cannot be bought for or exchanged into money or prizes.
Does it work offline?
Yes. After the page loads, the built-in question bank works without internet. Online AI questions and sharing need internet.
How many questions are there?
More than 1,200 built-in questions across GK, Science, Maths, English, Photo Quiz and Kids Fun, plus 100 levels.
Where is my progress saved?
On your own device (browser storage). If you clear your browser data, your progress is removed.
How do I challenge a friend?
Open Multiplayer Battle, play five questions and share the link. Your friend plays the same five questions and the scores are compared.
I found a wrong answer. What can I do?
Please tell us on the Contact page with the question text. We check every report.
Who makes the game?
NEXbrain Games. Read more on the About page.
