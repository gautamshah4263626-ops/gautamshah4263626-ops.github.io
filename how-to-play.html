How to Play AI QUIZ BATTLE

AI QUIZ BATTLE is a free quiz game for kids, teens and adults. This guide explains every part of the game so you can learn faster and score higher.

1. Choose who is playing

On your first visit you pick an age group: Kids (6–12), Teens (13–17) or Adults (18+). Kids get easy picture questions and a safer experience: sign-in, online leaderboard, invites, friend challenges and coin bets are switched off.

2. Pick a mode

Teacher Mode – no pressure. NEXbrain explains every answer, so this is the best mode for learning.
Battle Mode – you race against NEXbrain. Every question is a small duel.
Smart AI Mode – NEXbrain watches how you are doing. If you miss questions it becomes a teacher; if you are on a streak it becomes a rival.

3. Answer within 15 seconds

Each question has four options and a 15-second timer. After you answer you see the correct option, a short explanation and a fact. Correct answers earn coins; harder questions and answer streaks earn a few extra.

4. Levels 1 to 100

Level Mode has 100 levels. Each level has 5 questions. You need 3 correct answers to pass (4 correct from level 21 onwards). Five correct answers earn three stars. Beginner levels are 1–10, Pro 11–20, Master 21–30, Expert 31–50, Champion 51–75 and Legend 76–100.

5. Smart Learning Report

The report shows your accuracy in each subject, highlights weak topics and lets you practise exactly those topics. The mistakes you make are saved so you can review them later.

6. Bookmarks

After any answer, tap Save question to keep it. Open Bookmark Questions from the home screen to read your saved questions again or practise them as a quiz.

7. Shop and power-ups

Coins can be spent in the Shop on power-ups such as 50-50 (removes two wrong options), +5 seconds, Hint, Skip, Extra Life and Shield, or on avatar styles. Coins are only for fun inside the game; they have no cash value.

8. Friend Challenge

Play five questions, then share the link with a friend. Your friend plays the same five questions and the game compares both scores. It is a fair, simple way to compete without any account.
