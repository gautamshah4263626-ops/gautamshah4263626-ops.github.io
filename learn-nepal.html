Nepal General Knowledge

Nepal is a small country with a huge variety of geography and culture. These facts are often asked in exams and quizzes.

Basic facts

Capital: Kathmandu
Currency: Nepalese Rupee
Districts: 77
Provinces: 7
Form of government: Federal democratic republic (since 2008)
Constitution promulgated: 2015

Provinces and their capitals

Koshi – Biratnagar
Madhesh – Janakpur
Bagmati – Hetauda
Gandaki – Pokhara
Karnali – Birendranagar (Surkhet)

Lumbini Province and Sudurpashchim Province are the other two. Their capitals can change by decision, so check an updated source before an exam.

Mountains and rivers

Mount Everest (Sagarmatha), 8,848.86 m, lies on the border of Nepal and China and is the highest mountain in the world. Eight of the world's fourteen peaks above 8,000 m lie in Nepal or on its borders, including Kangchenjunga, Lhotse, Makalu, Cho Oyu, Dhaulagiri, Manaslu and Annapurna. The Karnali is the longest river inside Nepal. The Koshi, Gandaki and Karnali are the three major river systems.

National symbols

National flower: Rhododendron (Lali Gurans)
National bird: Danphe (Himalayan Monal)
National animal: Cow
National colour: Crimson red
The flag is the only non-rectangular national flag in the world.

History and culture

Gautam Buddha was born in Lumbini, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Pashupatinath Temple in Kathmandu is a famous Shiva temple. Pokhara is known for its lakes, and the Kathmandu Valley contains several UNESCO World Heritage Sites. In 1953 Tenzing Norgay and Edmund Hillary were the first to be confirmed at the top of Everest.
