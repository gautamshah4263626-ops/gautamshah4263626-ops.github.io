World General Knowledge

General knowledge helps in exams, interviews and everyday conversation. Here is a quick guide with facts that appear again and again in quizzes.

Continents and oceans

There are 7 continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia (Oceania). Asia is the largest and Australia is the smallest.
There are 5 oceans: Pacific (largest), Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic.

Countries

There are 193 member states of the United Nations. Russia is the largest country by area. Vatican City is the smallest. India and China have the largest populations. Many countries have a capital that is different from their biggest city, for example Australia (capital Canberra, not Sydney) and Brazil (capital Brasilia, not Rio de Janeiro).

Rivers, mountains and deserts

The Nile in Africa is commonly cited as the longest river, and the Amazon carries the most water. Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level. The Sahara is the largest hot desert, and Antarctica is the largest desert of all because it receives very little rain or snow. The deepest known point in the ocean is the Mariana Trench in the Pacific.

Space

There are 8 planets in our solar system. Jupiter is the largest and Mercury the smallest. Mars is called the Red Planet. In 1969 Neil Armstrong became the first person to walk on the Moon. Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth.

Science in daily life

Water is H₂O. The human body has 206 bones in adulthood. Plants make food by photosynthesis using sunlight, water and carbon dioxide. Our skin makes Vitamin D in sunlight.

Flags and currencies
