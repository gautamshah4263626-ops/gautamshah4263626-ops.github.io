Easy Maths Tricks for Fast Calculation

Maths becomes fun when you know a few shortcuts. Try these tricks, then test yourself in the Maths quizzes.

Multiply by 11

For a two-digit number, add the two digits and place the sum in the middle. Example: 23 × 11 → 2 _ 3 → 2 + 3 = 5 → 253. If the sum is 10 or more, carry the 1: 48 × 11 → 4 (4+8=12) 8 → 4+1, 2, 8 → 528.

The 9 times table on your fingers

Hold up both hands. To find 9 × 4, fold down the 4th finger. Fingers to the left (3) are the tens and fingers to the right (6) are the ones: 36.

Squares of numbers ending in 5

Take the first digit n, multiply it by (n + 1) and write 25 after it. Example: 35² → 3 × 4 = 12 → 1225. And 85² → 8 × 9 = 72 → 7225.

Percentages quickly

10% of a number: move the decimal one place (10% of 240 = 24).
50%: divide by 2. 25%: divide by 4. 5%: half of 10%.
15% of 80: 10% = 8, 5% = 4, total = 12.

Divisibility rules

By 2: last digit is even.
By 3: sum of digits is divisible by 3 (123 → 1+2+3 = 6 ✓).
By 5: last digit is 0 or 5.
By 9: sum of digits is divisible by 9.
By 10: last digit is 0.

Add big numbers by rounding

To compute 698 + 245, add 2 to 698 to make 700, then compute 700 + 245 = 945 and subtract the 2 you added: 943.

Check answers with digit sums

To check a multiplication, compare the digit sums. For 12 × 13 = 156: (1+2) × (1+3) = 3 × 4 = 12 → 1+2 = 3, and 1+5+6 = 12 → 3. They match, so the answer is likely right.
