Privacy Policy – AI QUIZ BATTLE

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This game is made by NEXbrain Games. Contact: gautamsah63f5@gmail.com

What we collect

  • On your device only: your game progress, coins, level, settings, nickname and the profile photo you choose. The photo is stored in your own browser or app and is never uploaded.
  • If you sign in with Google (Firebase Authentication): your Google account name, email address and a unique account ID. We use the ID to keep your score and invites tied to one account.
  • Online leaderboard: your nickname, total points, best score and number of games, saved in Google Firebase Realtime Database so other players can see the ranking.
  • Invites: an invite link carries your player ID so we can reward you when a friend joins.

Advertising

The website may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. Ads are not shown to players who choose the Kids age group.

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob (for example, an optional "watch an ad to earn coins" video). AdMob may use an advertising ID on your device. You can reset or limit it in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads. We do not sell your personal data.

Made for all ages (children, teens and adults)

When the game starts for the first time it asks "Who is playing?" (Kids 6–12, Teens 13–17, Adults 18+). This choice is stored only on your device. For Kids we turn on extra protection: Google sign-in, the online leaderboard, invite rewards, online questions, coin bets and paid spins are switched off; ads, if any, are set as child-directed, and we do not collect personal information from them. Teen and adult accounts that sign in with Google share the data described above.

Young children should choose their age group together with a parent or guardian. We do not ask for phone numbers, home addresses or real names. If you are a parent and believe your child's data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Coins have no cash value

Coins, levels and ranks are for fun only. They cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.

Your choices

  • Use Settings > Reset all data in the game to erase everything stored on your device.
  • To delete your online leaderboard entry and account data, email us from your account address and we will remove it.
  • You can play without signing in. Some features (online leaderboard and invite rewards) need sign-in.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS to Google Firebase. No method is 100% secure, so please do not put personal details in your nickname.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will appear at the top of this page.
